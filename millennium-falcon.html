Falcon YT-1300f Light Freighter

The most celebrated starship in the galaxy. Modified beyond all factory specification, this Corellian Engineering Corporation freighter has outrun Imperial Star Destroyers, survived the Kessel Run in 12 parsecs, and helped destroy two Death Stars.

Phase 01 — Foundation

Structural Spaceframe

Corellian Engineering's reinforced titanium-alloy lattice forms the backbone. Three primary bulkhead rings provide structural integrity under 8,700g load tolerances. The skeletal spaceframe is assembled in a pressurized orbital dock before atmospheric testing.

Core Propulsion & Drive

Twin Girodyne SRB42 overdrive units flank a central Isu-Sim SSP05 hyperdrive motivator. The custom-routed Class 0.5 hyperdrive — illegally modified from stock Class 2 — achieves jump entry velocities previously classified as physically impossible.
